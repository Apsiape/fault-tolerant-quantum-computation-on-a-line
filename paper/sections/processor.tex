\section{A fixed synchronous quantum processor}\label{sec:processor}
\begin{lemma}[A moving instruction stream]\label{lem:processor}
There is a fixed on-site word $U$ and a fixed exchange $E$ on finitely many opposite port pairs such that a programmed basis row of $B$ commands performs any width-$n$, depth-$D$ Toffoli/Hadamard circuit in $V$ native ticks, where
\begin{equation}\label{eq:interpretertime}
 B=(7n+8)(nD+1),\qquad V=2B.
\end{equation}
The stationary output qubits are at sites $1,\ldots,n$. The program, the head and all auxiliary inputs are computational-basis values. The same clean evolution acts on arbitrary input entangled with a reference. Output addresses and final time depend only on $n,D$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
A native site holds a stationary data qubit, a stream symbol, and a possible head with three quantum buffers and a finite tag. A command moves the head by one, exchanges buffer $r\in\{1,2,3\}$ with the stationary qubit, applies $H$ to buffer one, applies Toffoli to the three buffers in order, or waits. All commands except moves keep the head stationary. The head starts ready at site zero. A ready tick reads and executes the local symbol, followed by one refractory tick doing nothing before becoming ready. Symbols shift right by one each tick and are never erased. The refractory tick prevents a just-moved rightward head from reading the same symbol again. On valid states the tag depends only on the classical command history. Moving a buffer means transporting its quantum factor without measurement.

For each port pair let $E$ swap the right factor at $x$ with the left factor at $x+1$ on every bond. Different bonds use different internal factors, so the layer is a translated disjoint tensor product. Encode stream symbols with a fixed number of such pairs. Swapping left and right locally before $E$ makes the old left field shift right while the other field shifts left. Read the old left field.

Represent a head by a stationary record and two directional port records, each with empty, ready or refractory tags. At the start gather an occupied arriving record into the empty stationary record by controlled swaps. Compute controls on private copies of the old tags and instruction bits. For a valid one-head row exactly one record is occupied. Reset vacated records and their unused data; perform the stationary update; then keep the head stationary or send it into an empty prepared outgoing port. An arriving refractory head stays through the tick and becomes ready. A ready stay command sets the refractory tag. A site with no head leaves its data alone. Reset temporary controls after use.

A finite truth table with a fixed priority on malformed tags defines every branch. A conditional $H$ scatters its operand by controlled swaps to an exclusive dummy, applies unconditional $H$ and gathers. Conditional exchanges and Toffoli use fixed reversible Boolean gates with scratch. The elementary word, with resets, defines a channel on superposed tags as well; its asserted clean semantics needs only valid basis tags and no logical dephasing. All alphabets and records are finite, so this word has constant size. Enumerate internal slots and choose a lexicographically ordered direct truth-table compilation, serializing its gates. Fix one such word once. Let $d$ be the total number of port pairs, including one additional compiler pair. The remaining pairs are user-processor pairs.

For each target gate, start from home zero, visit its at most three distinct operand sites among $1,\ldots,n$, and swap their stationary data into the appropriate buffers. Apply $H$ or Toffoli on the buffers, revisit the operands to swap them back, and return home. Every operand returns to its own site and every buffer returns to zero. Seven traversals of length at most $n$ and seven stationary commands suffice. Order gates by layer, then least operand, retaining the declared control/target order. There are at most $nD$ gates. Pad by waits to the $B$ commands in \eqref{eq:interpretertime}.

Let $x_j$ be the head's ready position for command $j$ at time $2j$, for $0\le j<B$. It is computed from the motion list. Place command $j$ initially at stream site $x_j-2j$. These addresses are distinct: consecutive differences are $x_{j+1}-x_j-2\le-1$ because $|x_{j+1}-x_j|\le1$. At time $2j$ the rightward stream puts exactly the intended symbol under the ready head. Induct over commands. During the intervening tick the head is refractory, so no accidental second read occurs. At time $V=2B$ the stationary data contain exactly the output of $C$. All operations on data are the intended unitary gates or quantum swaps, so the induction is an operator statement and preserves an arbitrary reference. The auxiliary inputs, motion chart and output sites are fixed by the classical program or by $n,D$ as asserted.
\end{proof}

The logical program is protected in the recursive construction. It is not left on an unencoded physical tape. A parent has disjoint compiler slots and user-processor slots. Every coordinate of every parent slot, including user symbols, tags, buffers and ports, is stored on the children's compiler data tracks. The children's own copies of $U$ run on a distinct tensor factor and need no indication of the selected top rank.
