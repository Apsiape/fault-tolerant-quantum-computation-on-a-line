\section{Product loading and classical readout}\label{sec:boundary}
Write $\cC_d=\cD+de$. A block has classical input value $d$ with $f$ deviations when its computational-basis support lies in the span of words within Hamming distance $f$ of $\cC_d$. This permits arbitrary phases and purifications. Repeating a bit $d$ in all $N$ coordinates satisfies this condition with $f=0$, although such a product word generally lies outside the quantum-correctable span. Quantum deviations mean the operator span of errors supported on the stated number of coordinates applied to code support.

\begin{lemma}[Private teleportation normalizer]\label{lem:teleport}
There is a fixed native pass with the following properties, including arbitrary reference and environment coefficients. A clean pass maps every input into the code and acts as identity on encoded logical data. It takes classical input value $d$ with $f\le q$ deviations to $\ket{d_L}$. After $j$ faulty native locations, if $f+4j\le q$, quantum-correctable input retains its logical subsystem with at most $4j$ output deviations. Under the same inequality classical input value $d$ retains classical bit support within $4j$ of $\cC_d$, without any condition on its incoming phase syndrome.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Prepare fresh verified plus and zero blocks, and apply transversal $\CNOT$ from plus to zero, producing Bell halves $A,B$. Apply transversal $\CNOT$ with incoming block $X$ as control and $A$ as target, then transversal $H$ on $X$. Denote their computational-basis words by $u$ on $A$ and $v$ on $X$. Before private calculations, copy each raw word coordinatewise into a separate record for every output coordinate. Compute the total function $a$ privately on each copy. At output coordinate $r$, apply $X$ to $B_r$ controlled by its private $a(u)$ and $Z$ controlled by its private $a(v)$. Keep the original raw records until disposal. Fixed swaps move $B$ to the output slots. A private computation is discarded after its one output coordinate, or reversibly uncomputed. Copies, switches, waits, resets, swaps and disposal are ordinary locations; no branch changes a schedule.

In a clean pass the prepared $B$ has code support. Conditional on any raw words it still has code support for every input, because the other Bell half alone interacted with $X$. Clean frame bits are identical at all coordinates, so the corrections are uniform transversal logical Paulis and every input is normalized.

For logical input $\ket{l_L}$ and Bell logical value $c$, the branch amplitude multiplying $\ket{c_L}$ is a fixed positive normalization times
\begin{equation}\label{eq:bellcharacter}
 \sum_{\substack{x\in\cC_l\\x+u\in\cC_c}}(-1)^{v\cdot x}.
\end{equation}
It vanishes unless $u\in\cC_{l+c}$ and $v\in\cD^\perp=\cC$. In the remaining case it equals $|\cD|(-1)^{l a(v)}$: $e$ is odd and perpendicular to $\cD$. Thus each nonzero uncorrected logical branch is a common scalar times $X_L^{a(u)}Z_L^{a(v)}$ on the input. Undoing this frame changes only a branch scalar. This is an operator identity on the logical qubit, hence holds with any reference.

For classical input, expand $X$ in basis words $x$. If the Bell logical value is $c$, then $u=x+y$ with $y\in\cC_c$. For $x$ within $f$ of $\cC_d$, $u$ is within $f$ of $\cC_{d+c}$. When $f\le q$, the bit-frame decision is $d+c$. After that frame $B$ has support on $\cC_d$, regardless of the phase frame. In a clean pass it also has code support, whose intersection with this basis support is precisely the line $\mathbb C\ket{d_L}$. The argument is termwise for arbitrary superpositions of the allowed words and arbitrary joint coefficients.

We now count faults after routing and compiler decisions have been deleted. Include preparation faults in $j$. The verified preparation statement in Section~\ref{sec:code} says that a preparation with $k\le q$ faults has terms with at most $k$ affected coordinates. Faults on the transversal $H$ for plus add only their own coordinates. Expand all other faults in local matrix or Pauli bases, retaining their shared environment coefficients. Up to the private frame calculations, the couplings, raw copies, swaps and Hadamards outside preparation are Clifford and coordinatewise. A fault on Bell coupling, input coupling or Hadamard remains at its one index across the affected blocks. A wait touches one token. A raw-copy fault may alter any subset of copies at that index but cannot alter another index. Treat the records as quantum basis controls until disposal, or expand at the raw-record cut in computational-basis matrix units. Keeping the original records makes their phases branch coefficients.

Each private decoder has its own input copy and touches just its declared output coordinate. A corrupt private computation cannot reach another decoder's input or target. A faulty private calculation, frame application or final swap leaves at most its declared output coordinate arbitrary. Subsequent copying or disposal faults expand on their named factors. Every private work factor is reset before use, so old garbage does not add a channel to another target. Verified-preparation deviations, shifts of raw words and arbitrary output targets together occupy at most $4j$ indices in each term. This is a conservative count: every listed fault can be charged to its own coordinate index, but no sharper count is needed. An arbitrarily long fanout only repeats the same raw index. Geometric shuttles do not enter this count because they have already been deleted into the native location budget.

For a quantum input with $f$ deviations, expand the incoming deviations in Paulis. Through Bell extraction such a Pauli shifts $u,v$ at its supported indices and supplies phases. Under $f+4j\le q$, every intact private decoder chooses the corresponding unshifted ideal logical frame by uniqueness of bounded-distance decoding. The output in that term is the intended logical state with an operator on at most $4j$ coordinates. To see this for coherent faults, project on the true raw words, use the logical identity \eqref{eq:bellcharacter}, and expand each affected private output in a single-qubit matrix basis. Scalar or environment coefficients can depend on the raw words and fault term, but the clean branch on the logical subsystem is proportional to identity. The conclusion therefore holds on the entire correctable span jointly with the reference.

For classical input, only the $u$ calculation is required to select $d$. Every shift in an intact $u$ decoder costs at most $4j$ indices, and $f+4j\le q$ still forces its answer to be $d+c$ for every contributing $x,y,c$. Different phase-frame decisions at all positions merely change basis coefficients. Bell errors and arbitrary output targets occupy at most the charged $4j$ indices. The output support is consequently within $4j$ of $\cC_d$ irrespective of the incoming phase syndrome. Taking sums of fault paths preserves this subspace assertion.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Aligned loading at every rank]\label{lem:loading}
Fix $b\ge1$ and $q-8\ge160b+64$. Before the ordinary $b+1$ leading full error-correction passes of every epoch, perform $b+1$ private teleportation passes on each slot, finishing all normalizers before an interslot body. With at most $b$ effective native faults in a good parent, a repeated classical bit becomes the required encoded basis value. Correctable quantum input with at most $9b$ incoming deviations retains its logical value. Arbitrary input normalizes to some logical subsystem before interslot gates. These assertions hold jointly and can be iterated from the physical product boundary through every reduction rank. Every physical quantum input can be zero.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Count touching faults on fresh work, data, waits between lists and final transport in the total $b$. Among $b+1$ passes on a slot one has no touching fault. Before that pass, the classical-support statement of Lemma~\ref{lem:teleport}, applied successively, leaves at most $4b+b$ deviations, including at most $b$ wait deviations. The inequality needed at every stage is $f+4j\le9b\le q$. The clean pass puts the slot on the desired line $\mathbb C\ket{d_L}$. Later teleportations use the quantum clause with at most $4b+b$ deviations and preserve the chosen logical state.

For ordinary correctable quantum input, allow $9b$ incoming deviations and $b$ waits. Each pass meets $f+4j\le14b\le q$; after the chain at most $5b$ deviations remain. For arbitrary input, a clean pass has code support for some logical subsystem, and later passes obey the same quantum estimate. The clean pass on each slot may occur at a different date; all of them finish before interslot bodies.

These are joint channel assertions. To make their meaning explicit, choose the fixed recovery coordinates $W$ of \eqref{eq:recoverycoordinates}. On a valid logical matrix unit with syndrome coefficient $Y$, a clean private pass has
\[
 (W\otimes I)\mathcal T\!\left(
 W^\dagger(|a\rangle\langle a'|\otimes Y)W\right)(W^\dagger\otimes I)
   =|a\rangle\langle a'|\otimes\Lambda_{\mathcal T}(Y),
\]
where identities on exterior factors are understood and $\Lambda_{\mathcal T}$ is independent of $a,a'$. This follows by multiplying the Bell-branch scalar for $a$ by its conjugate for $a'$: the corrected branch is the same scalar times identity. For classical input value $d$ the logical factor on the right is instead $|d\rangle\langle d|$, with the coefficient obtained from the arbitrary incoming allowed basis-word matrix units. On arbitrary input the clean pass is $V\mathcal N(\,\cdot\,)V^\dagger$ for a total selecting channel $\mathcal N$ on that block only. Its absorption into a preceding named map is the explicit composition \eqref{eq:badabsorb}. Section~\ref{sec:compiler} proves \eqref{eq:jointcut} for full semantic operations, traces port support and gives the rank-zero and both date-zero induction steps. Ordinary error correction and bodies, with subsequent waits counted in $b$, return its $9b$ cut allowance.

To initialize, replace every top basis bit by $N$ identical bits at the declared child coordinates for all $S$ parent slots. Use fixed zero defaults on unassigned work, and repeat down to the physical row. This is a classical substitution, with no quantum encoding. A good rank-one initial epoch meets Lemma~\ref{lem:teleport}'s classical-support hypothesis. A bad epoch is an arbitrary operation on that same specified input, including replacement. After one rank reduction the specified time-zero row is again exactly the repeated-bit row: a good initial instruction has its intended action on it, whereas a bad initial instruction is named at its native site and port images. At positive dates use the correctable-or-arbitrary invariant. The fault-free equivalence of Lemma~\ref{lem:deletion} holds on arbitrary old port contents and excluded scratch, including after a wholly bad preceding epoch. With named faults present, deletion gives that compiled list with at most $b$ charged native faults in a good parent; Lemma~\ref{lem:teleport} and the preceding normalization argument apply with that budget. Induct on rank, using this date-zero case anew for each reduced circuit. No assumption says all physical input blocks are good. For recursively encoded quantum input the induction instead uses the quantum clause from the start.

Finally keep a separate classical pulse bit for every physical quantum slot. Prepare the slot in zero and the pulse bit in its prescribed repeated input value. At the start of every physical period apply a $\CNOT$ from that bit to its slot, then reset the pulse bit unconditionally to zero. Noiselessly the first pulse loads the basis value and every later pulse is the identity. A wrong later pulse requires a flag since its most recent reset, within a stencil of at most two periods. Include faults on the initial copy, reset, following noise and storage in the direct physical tests. No upper-rank instruction reads a pulse bit. Work pulses may all be zero. Thus all physical quantum slots start in zero while only classical basis registers contain the nonzero input description.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Total classical measurement decoder]\label{lem:readout}
There is a total fixed rule $g:\{0,1\}^N\to\{0,1\}$ such that measuring a block in the computational basis and applying $g$ has exactly the same effects as applying the fixed CSS recovery and measuring its logical qubit in $Z$, on the entire Hilbert space. Bottom-up application gives the full joint law of recursively recovered logical output measurements, including entangled and bad intermediate blocks.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For each bit syndrome and each phase syndrome separately, choose a least-weight representative with lexicographic tie break. Two $X$ parts of weight at most $q$ with the same syndrome differ by a word of $\cC$ of weight at most $2q$, so they are equal by $d(\cC)\ge2q+1$. The same holds for $Z$ parts. Each syndrome sector of the low-weight error span therefore contains one Pauli up to phase, and this total table corrects the entire span. It is an admissible instance of the total recovery tables of~\cite[Lemma~5.1]{DMMemory2026}. Fix this table as the recovery at every recursive cut of~\cite[Lemma~13.1]{DMMemory2026}.

On a measured word $w$, take the bit syndrome of the $Z$ checks, subtract its chosen leader, and return the coset in $\cC/\cD$. This is $g$; inside the bounded-distance region it agrees with $a$. For a fixed bit syndrome and logical answer, the corresponding corrected classical subset is a translate of $\cD$. Its diagonal projector commutes with the $X$ stabilizers, which translate by $\cD$, with the $Z$ stabilizers, and with the diagonal phase correction. Thus in the recovered logical-$Z$ POVM the phase-syndrome measurement drops out on summation. The bit correction translates the subset by its chosen leader. On code support these are exactly the two logical-$Z$ projectors; on the full space the same summation gives the diagonal effects $\sum_{w:g(w)=d}\ket w\bra w$. This proves the claimed full-space effect identity.

For several blocks take tensor products of these effects. Their equality as operators gives the joint measurement law even for entangled blocks. Replacing each next-level recovered measurement by its diagonal physical effect and inducting from the top down is precisely bottom-up application of $g$ to the measured words. The identity applies even to bad children, so no correctability premise is inserted at an intermediate measurement cut. Read only declared block coordinates. Tracing out garbage is equivalent to ignoring its measured bits. All tables have constant finite size, and the recovery convention is the one used at every reduction cut.
\end{proof}
